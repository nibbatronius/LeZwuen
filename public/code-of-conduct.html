Code of Conduct

LeZwuen is a local-first forum. Keep conversations respectful, safe, and community-focused.

  1. Be respectful and assume good intent.
  2. No buy, sell, or trade posts in Nearby rooms.
  3. Do not share precise locations or personal data.
  4. Report harmful behavior or unsafe content promptly.
  5. Respect blocks; do not evade or harass.

If you see something concerning, use the Report action in chat.
