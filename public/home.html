Mushroom cloning process

Make agar

Let it fully solidify

Cut a small mycelium wedge (rice-grain size)

Place it on top of the agar

Close and incubate

This allows:

Radial outward growth

Visual identification of contamination

Clean edge selection for future transfers

This is the standard method worldwide.

Preparing substrate:
Steps

Add 1 part dry coco coir to bucket

Add 4 parts boiling water

Cover 1–2 hours

Break up and mix

Squeeze test (field capacity):

Few drops → ✅ ready

Streams → ❌ too wet

No drops, crumbly → ❌ too dry

Let cool → use

Fixes

Too wet:

Squeeze harder by hand

Spread thin for 10–20 min, then re-mix

Too dry:

Add small splashes of clean water

Mix and re-test

Notes

Moist, not dripping

Cool before inoculating

No nutrients needed

Done.
